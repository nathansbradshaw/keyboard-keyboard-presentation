This is the concrete open-hardware example. The current keyboard PCB already routes Daisy AUDIO_OUT_1 and AUDIO_OUT_2 to J2, while the current audio callback is pass-through. The proposed next step is to prototype SparkFun BOB-11044, then adapt the relevant TPA2005D1 circuit into our board. SparkFun specifies 2.5 to 5.5 volts, up to 1.4 watts into eight ohms, differential inputs that may also be ground-referenced, a shutdown input, and an optional ten-kilohm volume pot. Its class-D output switches around 250 kilohertz, so SparkFun says to keep the amplifier near the speaker. The speaker must connect across OUT+ and OUT−; neither output is speaker ground. OSHWA certification US001040 lists CERN for hardware, MIT for software, and CC BY-SA for documentation. We should preserve attribution, the applicable license notices, and release our modified source as required.
