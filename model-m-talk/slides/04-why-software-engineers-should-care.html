制約は邪魔じゃない。 The constraint gives the work its character.

Reveal the three manga panels one beat at a time: pixel art, watercolor, then embedded. Invite software engineers to see embedded development as a creative medium, not merely software with fewer resources. Pixel art gets its character from a finite grid and palette. Watercolor gets its character from working with water, pigment, paper, and imperfect control. Embedded systems ask us to work with clocks, memory, power, mechanical geometry, analog noise, and physical tolerances. Those limits force deliberate choices and make the result distinctive. In this project, the medium includes an old shell, one hundred analog sensors, a one-millisecond scan, and DIN MIDI. Transition: if MIDI keyboards are already solved, why deliberately choose these constraints?
