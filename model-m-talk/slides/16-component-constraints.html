妥協は設計 The best part is the one that works.
Parts catalogs are hostile to open-ended browsing. Before searching, turn the project into filters. Separate hard constraints from preferences: the part must run at the system voltage, expose the needed signal, physically fit, and be orderable. Noise, bandwidth, package size, supplier, and price may be negotiable depending on the prototype. Stock and assembly support are design constraints too; a perfect part that cannot be purchased or placed is not perfect. Narrow the field first, then compare the remaining datasheets. For keyboard_keyboard, that process selected the MT9105ET linear Hall-effect sensor for all one hundred keys. Expect compromise. We often trade a little sensitivity, board area, cost, or hand-solderability to get the combination that actually ships. Transition: once the shortlist is small, read the remaining datasheets as the component contracts.
