MODEL M ADAPTATIONThe computer-keyboard stagger stays physical. The Wicki–Hayden relationship lives in software.
Isomorphic means that a musical interval keeps the same geometric relationship wherever it starts. In the Wicki–Hayden layout, each row advances by whole steps. Adjacent rows supply the other whole-tone set. In this project's exact firmware table, adjacent playable rows are offset by a perfect fourth. A chord or scale fingering can therefore move to a new root without changing its internal shape. Do not overclaim that every immediate diagonal on our staggered Model M is a semitone; that shortcut does not match the actual SWITCH_TO_NOTE table.
