IDEA+KiCad+A REFERENCE→YOUR KEYBOARD

* Stealing is a joke, please don't actually steal, respect the license and original creators

Most custom keyboards begin with three things. First is the idea: a layout, a constraint, or one weird interaction you wish existed. Second is a board design tool such as KiCad, which turns that idea into a schematic, PCB, and fabrication files. Third is a proven reference: an existing circuit or mechanical design whose working pieces you can study and adapt. The “STEAL” label is a joke, not advice to ignore ownership. Respect the original creators and verify the exact license before copying anything. Transition: meet the two open-source parents of this keyboard.
