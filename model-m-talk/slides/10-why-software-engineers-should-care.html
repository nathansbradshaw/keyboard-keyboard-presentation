制約は邪魔じゃない。 Constraints make the project.

Reveal the three manga panels one beat at a time: pixel art, chiptune, then embedded. Invite software engineers to see embedded development as a creative medium, not merely software with fewer resources. Pixel art gets its character from a finite grid and palette. Chiptune gets its recognizable sound from a small number of voices, simple waveforms, and tiny memory budgets. Embedded systems ask us to work with clocks, memory, power, mechanical geometry, analog noise, and physical tolerances. Those limits force deliberate choices and make the result distinctive. In this project, the medium includes an old shell, one hundred analog sensors, a one-millisecond scan, and DIN MIDI. Transition: if MIDI keyboards are already solved, why deliberately choose these constraints?
