制約は邪魔じゃない。 Constraints make the project.

0. Embeded Development is an art of contraints. As we are limited in what we can do, we often build something increadable. 1. Think like pixel art. Every pixel is a deliberate choice. That choice is what makes the image look good. 2. Chiptune started as a simple sound synthesis engine. The constraint of the systems playing chiptunes is what made that sound so iconic. We can produce better sounds now, but the style has grown beyond the original constraint. 3. Embeded systems apply a constraint on software that we don't usually get to experience in the copreate business world. Every function has to be intentional, every variable a contionus choice, it allows us to build software art of the likes of pixel and chiptune art.
