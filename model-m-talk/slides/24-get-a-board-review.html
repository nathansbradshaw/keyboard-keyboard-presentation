AI RULEUse it to generate questions—not confidence.
Run the automated checks, then ask a human to review the schematic, layout, BOM, and fabrication preview. A design-rule checker proves that the layout follows the rules it was given; it cannot prove the rules or the circuit are correct. A reviewer can challenge assumptions about current paths, analog return, decoupling, connector orientation, assembly access, and whether the board can be tested. If no human is available, an AI review is still useful for generating a checklist, noticing inconsistent labels, or asking about missing protection. But treat every AI statement as an unverified review comment. It may invent a pin function, confuse package variants, recommend unnecessary changes, or miss a fatal issue. Require evidence: exact datasheet page, fabricator rule, calculation, or measurement. The final authority remains the primary documentation and accountable engineering judgment. Close the field guide with the preflight: power, polarity, footprints, connectors, test points, and fab rules—then order a small batch.
