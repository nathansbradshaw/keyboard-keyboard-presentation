OUR ADAPTATION

Model M MIDI instrument

The Model M decision looked like a massive measurement and PCB task until mod-mmm provided a mechanical starting point. HE60 provided an open Hall-effect electrical reference. These photographs show the real parent projects beside the finished adaptation: the curve comes from the Model M problem, while the analog sensing model comes from a Hall-effect keyboard. This is normal open-source engineering: understand both sources, then adapt them for a different geometry, controller, channel count, firmware, and musical purpose. mod-mmm photograph by dcpedit from the CC BY 4.0 mod-mmm repository. HE60 photograph by peppapighs from the GPL-3.0 HE60 repository. Transition: at this point, a software engineer may reasonably ask why any of this matters.
