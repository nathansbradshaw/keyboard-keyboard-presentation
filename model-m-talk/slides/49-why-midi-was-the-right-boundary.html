Why MIDI was the right boundary

Keyboard
owns intent
→
MIDI
owns the contract
→
Phone
owns sound
Separation of concerns
Interchangeable parts
Inspectable messages
Clear responsibility
MIDI is the API boundary. The keyboard repository proves what it sends; the phone must prove how it turns those messages into sound. That separation let both systems evolve independently and made debugging possible with standard tools. Transition: extract lessons from the whole system.
