New achievements unlocked

U1 enable tied high

74HC138 pin 5 is active-low—but the PCB connects it to +3.3 V.

Impact: AM10–AM13 stay disabled.

Hotfix: bridge pad 5 → pad 4 (GND).

Daisy pad 33 has no ADC

The AM14/AM15 pot path landed on a non-ADC pin.

Impact: twelve control pots cannot be read.

Hotfix: wire pad 33 → pad 35 (A11).

These are the two documented board errata. U1 pin 5 disables HE71–HE100 until bridged. The Daisy pad bodge restores the A11 path for AM14/AM15 and twelve pots. Reveal a triumphant Ren only after both hotfixes land. Transition: software engineers already know these patterns by different names.
