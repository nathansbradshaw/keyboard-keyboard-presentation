Electrical intent

A schematic is the high-level electrical design of the project. You place symbols for the components you need, then define how those components connect to one another. The result is easy to read: you can follow a signal through the system and understand the whole design without thinking about where anything physically sits on the board. This is the real keyboard_keyboard schematic, with MT9102ET Hall sensors connected to SN74LV4051A multiplexers. It describes electrical intent, not physical layout. Transition: the PCB editor takes this readable plan and turns it into physical placement and copper.
