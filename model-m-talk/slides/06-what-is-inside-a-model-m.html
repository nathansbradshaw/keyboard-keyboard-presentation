The Model M uses a buckling spring, this is the precurser to the Mechanical switches we have today. This is what makes the Model M so unique. But is also the big issue. To satisfy the loud soft requrement we need at least 2 key presses from 1 key. With the buckle springs we don't know how fast the user is pressing the keys.
