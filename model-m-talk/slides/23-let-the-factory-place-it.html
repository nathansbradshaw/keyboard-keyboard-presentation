Design for the factory you plan to use. Stock · orientation · footprint · substitutions
A compact board can contain parts that are technically hand-solderable but strategically silly to hand-solder. Once the design uses many small passives, fine-pitch packages, or no-lead packages such as QFN, ask the fabricator to assemble it. You provide a verified BOM and component placement file in addition to the fabrication files. EasyEDA is a low-friction route because the editor connects component records and LCSC part numbers to JLCPCB's ordering and SMT assembly flow. This is convenience, not permission to stop checking: EasyEDA's own documentation warns that library entries can be wrong. Verify every symbol, footprint, package, pin one, polarity, stock status, and the assembly preview. If one specialized connector or switch is not supported, let the fab place the tiny commodity parts and hand-install the exception. Sources: https://easyeda.com/ · https://docs.easyeda.com/en/Schematic/Libraries/ · https://prodocs.easyeda.com/en/pcb/order-order-parts/ . Transition: tools and factories help, but communities prevent expensive mistakes that automated checks cannot recognize.
