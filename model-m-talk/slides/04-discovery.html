0. This lead to the first issue, How do you make a keyboard sing? An I am not talking about the click clack of you cherry blue lovers out there, How do you make it sing midi? 1. Our intial though was do USB to midi, we could build out a converter to turn the serial into midi. It would be easy, universal and cheap. It probably already existed, but it wasn't what we wanted. 2. Our next thought was to build a custom MIDI interface from scratch. This would give us more control and flexibility over the MIDI output, but would mean we had to build it ourselves. 3. regardless of what we did, the solution had to have touch response, expression. Piano Forte, it had to be lowd and soft from the same key.
