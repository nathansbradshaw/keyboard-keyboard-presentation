Webster's dictionary defines YOINK as the act of taking what is legally the public's and making it your own. You can do this exact thing with hardware projects just like you can with open source software projects. So there's actually a certification program called Oshawa where you can find certified projects that you can look at the schematics, you can copy the designs, you can find everything you need to know about the project and put it in your own design. And so here we have several different companies that actually post a lot of their products under the Oshawa certified projects certification in which you can actually buy something a lot of things from Spark Fund and have and then take that design and copy it directly onto your PCB.
