The note lookup in the previous slide is not an arbitrary list. It carries a musical keyboard idea first patented by Kaspar Wicki for bandoneon in 1896, then independently conceived and refined by concertina player Brian Hayden, who patented his arrangement in 1986. On the left is a real 46-button Hayden duet concertina. On the right is our real Model M conversion. We did not copy the concertina's physical button board. We borrowed the note relationships and encoded them in SWITCH_TO_NOTE while preserving the Model M's staggered rows.
