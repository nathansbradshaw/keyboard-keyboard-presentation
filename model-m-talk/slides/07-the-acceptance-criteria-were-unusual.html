制約が設計になった。 The constraints did not decorate the architecture. They created it.

This is the point of the slide: requirements are causal. Preserving the shell forced the board geometry. Expressive input forced analog sensing. Playable velocity forced calibration and a deterministic scan. Connecting to the external Synth Phone made MIDI the boundary. The numbers come from the real repository: 100 MT9105ET sensors across 13 SN74LV4051A muxes, 64 calibration samples per switch, a 1 kHz timer task, and USART1 MIDI at 31250 baud. Transition: before choosing Hall effect, test the simplest way to estimate key speed.
