Different manufacturers. One cable. It worked.

メーカーを越えて、音楽がつながる。 MIDI was an interoperability agreement before it was a cable.
In a world of musical discord, there were five synthesizer companies. And synthesizers were young, wild, and free. They didn't play nice with each other. They didn't even talk with each other. All of these companies building synthesizers with ways to only talk to themselves. Rowland found that the lack of standardization was limiting the growth of the electronic music industry and proposed a standard. Later, five companies got together to develop this standard. Roland, Yamaha, Korg, Kauai, and Sisquential all had their own five Kage summit where they discussed the rules on how musical instrf instruments will interface with each other. And so MIDI was born.
