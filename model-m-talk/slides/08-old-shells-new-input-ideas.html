答えは交差点にあった。 The instrument emerged at the intersection—not inside any one reference.

This is not a mood board. It is a design genealogy. The Model M answered form and presence. Wicki-Hayden answered note relationships. Two-stage mechanisms suggested measuring velocity from elapsed travel time. Hall-effect boards suggested continuous position. Synth and arcade panels informed direct controls and feedback. Each answer also created a cost that the project had to absorb. Credit specific external projects such as Melodicade MX when named verbally or when their imagery is used.
