Two digital edges turn time into a velocity proxy.

WE OBSERVE

Two timestamps with a fixed mechanical separation

WE LOSE

Position before, between and after those trip points

ERROR COMES FROM

Alignment, switch tolerance, bounce and changing Δx

Explain the measurement, not just the mechanism. If the two switches are separated by a known travel distance delta-x, timestamp their edges at t1 and t2 and use delta-x divided by delta-t as a velocity proxy. It is simple and entirely digital. But it observes only two points on the key trajectory. Mechanical spacing, alignment, tolerance, and bounce become measurement error, while continuous position and aftertouch remain invisible. This was an early concept, not the final implementation. Transition: evaluate those costs explicitly.
