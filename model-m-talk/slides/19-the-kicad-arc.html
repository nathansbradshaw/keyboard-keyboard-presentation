Next: follow the board through each stage.
Before we dive into the buttons and shortcuts, here is the whole KiCad journey. KiCad is the tool, and we will look at three stages of the same board. First, the schematic records electrical intent: what connects to what. Second, the PCB turns that logic into physical footprints, placement, and copper. Third, Gerber files package the reviewed board as manufacturing instructions a factory can build. We will follow those stages in context, pausing before the Gerber handoff for assembly planning and review. But before placing the first schematic symbol, complete the electronics preflight: narrow the component choices, read their contracts, and prove the riskiest behavior.
