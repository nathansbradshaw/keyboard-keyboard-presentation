The type records what each pin became.

Cargo selected the board; now main.rs claims its real pins. Daisy D7, D8, and D9 become push-pull outputs named s0, s1, and s2. Those names correspond directly to the three mux-select nets on the schematic. take() also gives the program exclusive ownership: another part of the firmware cannot quietly claim the same pin. The following ADC setup does the same thing for Daisy D15 through D23, converting them into analog inputs. Transition: these files now have clear jobs, and main.rs coordinates their one-way flow.
