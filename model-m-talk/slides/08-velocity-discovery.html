This is the discovery payoff. First, Melodicade MX showed that an expressive instrument could estimate velocity with two stacked switch events: the known distance between them and the elapsed time from t1 to t2 give a speed estimate. Then Hall effect switches gave us a different model: the Gateron KS-20 exploded view makes the mechanism visible. The magnet rides inside the moving stem; the Hall sensor sits on the PCB beneath it and exposes key position as an analog signal throughout the press. These were two separate leads, not the same mechanism. Together they changed our question from “did the key click?” to “how did the key move?” Melodicade MX photo and project reference: KOOP Instruments, https://www.koopinstruments.com/instrument-projects/melodicade-mx.
