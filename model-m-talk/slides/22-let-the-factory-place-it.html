Pick your factory before you finish the board. Its parts and rules will shape the design.
Some parts are technically hand-solderable. That does not mean you want to place them a hundred times. Once the board has lots of tiny passives, fine-pitch parts, or a QFN, pay the factory to do that work. Along with the board files, send a checked BOM and a placement file. EasyEDA makes the path to JLCPCB fairly direct because its parts link to LCSC inventory. You still need to check the symbols, footprints, pin one, polarity, stock, and the assembly preview—library parts can be wrong. If the factory cannot place a special connector or switch, let it handle the fiddly parts and solder the exception yourself. Sources: https://easyeda.com/ · https://docs.easyeda.com/en/Schematic/Libraries/ · https://prodocs.easyeda.com/en/pcb/order-order-parts/ .
