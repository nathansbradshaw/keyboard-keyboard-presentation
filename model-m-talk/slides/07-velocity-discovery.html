0. So we did some research into how we can turn one keypress into loud/soft, piano/forte by measuring the velocity of the key press. This is how electronic midi keyboards do it. 1. Our research led us to the Melodicade MX, a midi instrument that uses 2 stacked switches to measure key velocity. This clever solution allows for a single small key to measure velocity. As an asie this also is what lead us to use the wikihayden design. more on that later. 2. Some people in the ZMK discord suggested using a Hall Effect sensor, whixh allows us to define two actuation points for velocity sensing. The downside is that we'd have to build HE keyboard from scratch with no expierence on using HE and not many examples of HE keyboards.
