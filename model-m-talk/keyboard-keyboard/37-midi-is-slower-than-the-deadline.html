Queue 64: scan now, send later.

The snippets are condensed from timer_handler, process_events, and midi.rs. DIN MIDI uses USART1 at 31,250 baud. In the configured UART framing, each byte occupies a start bit, eight data bits, and a stop bit. A three-byte NoteOn therefore occupies 30 wire bits, or about 0.96 milliseconds. That is an inferred serialization time, not measured end-to-end latency, but it is already almost the entire scan period. A chord makes the conflict obvious. The priority-15 task queues intent into a heapless 64-entry SPSC queue; the priority-2 task drains it and may block on UART. Fixed capacity avoids a heap, but enqueue currently discards its error, so a production revision should count and expose overflows. For your design, calculate transport time before choosing queue depth, and instrument every drop path. Transition: after timing is isolated, tables keep the PCB geometry out of the musical policy.
