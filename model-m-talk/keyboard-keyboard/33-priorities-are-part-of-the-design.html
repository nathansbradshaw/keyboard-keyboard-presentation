速い処理より、予測できる処理 Real-time means bounded—not merely fast.
The priority numbers come directly from the RTIC task attributes in main.rs. TIM2 is priority 15 because missing that deadline makes the physical scan irregular. The DMA audio interrupt is priority 8. Event routing and blocking MIDI output run at priority 2. OLED drawing is priority 1 because stale UI is preferable to stale input. RTIC also forces resources into local ownership or explicit shared access, which makes interrupt safety visible in the type structure. For your keyboard, list every recurring job, its maximum acceptable delay, and its worst-case execution time before assigning priorities. Do not put logging, display I/O, allocation, or serial backpressure in the scan path. Transition: the first scan cannot mean anything until each physical sensor has a reference point.
