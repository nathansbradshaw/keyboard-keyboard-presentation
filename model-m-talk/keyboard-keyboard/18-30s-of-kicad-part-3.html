✓ The design becomes manufacturing instructions. 発注!
The fabrication handoff has three beats. First, plot the manufacturing layers and drill data from KiCad: copper, mask, silkscreen, outline, paste, and holes. Second, inspect the uploaded Gerbers rather than assuming the editor view is what the factory received. Confirm the outline is closed, drills are visible, layers align, and the upload is the intended revision. Third, check the order itself. This project used two-layer, 1.6 mm FR-4 at 447.04 by 162.81 millimeters; the shown quote is $44.90 for five boards before shipping. The joke is that clicking Order turns design decisions into expensive physical facts. Transition: keep the workflow manageable by reducing KiCad to seven recurring questions.
