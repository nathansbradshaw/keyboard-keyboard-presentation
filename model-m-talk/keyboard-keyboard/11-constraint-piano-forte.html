HE position + elapsed time → MIDI velocity 1–127

A Hall sensor reports continuously as the magnet moves with the key. The firmware does not need an infinite set of thresholds; it chooses two fixed positions and measures the elapsed time between them. A slow press takes longer to cross that distance and maps to a low MIDI velocity. A fast press crosses quickly and maps to a high velocity. The 70 ms / velocity 18 and 8 ms / velocity 116 examples make that inverse relationship visible; the production mapping spans MIDI velocity 1 through 127.
