REAL POST Phantom keys · 100 Hall sensors · 3.3 V rail sag → Engagement + practical insight from all three
I did not just find communities. I found reviewers.
This is firsthand, not a generic list of recommendations. I took the same real board problem to Embedded Engineering, Daisy Seed, and Keyboard Atelier, and got engagement and useful insight from all three communities. The problem was phantom keypresses on some builds. I reported that the MT9105ET supply measured roughly 2.44 to 2.8 volts on the failing boards, versus 2.71 to 3.16 volts on the working board, while the datasheet specifies a 3-volt minimum. I included the roughly 700-milliamp draw seen when the keyboard and Daisy ran correctly from a 3.3-volt bench supply, plus the LM317 test that measured 3.33 volts unloaded but fell to 2.5 volts when connected to the PCB. I also linked the KiCanvas schematic and explained the cuts, bodges, and tests already attempted. That specificity gave people something concrete to reason about. ZMK remains here as another strong keyboard community, but the three “Asked here” cards are the groups used to troubleshoot this board. Be a good community member: search first, redact secrets, ask a narrow question, and return with the outcome.
