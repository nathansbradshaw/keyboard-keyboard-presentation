Before we dive into the buttons and shortcuts, here is the whole KiCad journey. KiCad is the tool, and we will look at three stages of the same board. First, the schematic records electrical intent: what connects to what. Second, the PCB turns that logic into physical footprints, placement, and copper. Third, Gerber files package the finished board as manufacturing instructions a factory can build. The next three slides take one quick pass through each stage: schematic, PCB, then Gerber. Transition: start with the schematic—the board before it has a physical location.
