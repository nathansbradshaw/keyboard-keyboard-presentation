That Time We Turned an IBM Model M into a MIDI Instrument

to meet Mark Rober · electroNuck × N8

Open with the title and the object, not the implementation. This is the second attempt to build something interesting enough to meet Mark Rober, and this time the unreasonable input device is an IBM Model M turned into a velocity-sensitive MIDI instrument. Introduce electroNuck and N8, then move quickly into last year’s failed quest. Do not explain Hall sensors, the PCB, or the firmware yet; the discovery arc earns those details.
