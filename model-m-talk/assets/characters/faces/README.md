# Reaction face catalog

The established Mio and Ren emotions are available at three intensities:

- `emotion.png` — restrained, flexible reaction
- `emotion-big.png` — stronger slide-readable acting
- `emotion-max.png` — maximum comedic overreaction

All files are text-free transparent PNGs with simple TV-anime cel rendering and flat color. The established Mio and Ren sets also use a narrow off-white separation edge.

## Mio (美緒)

An original character with dusty-rose hair, gray-violet eyes, and a cream sweatshirt:

- `confident`
- `confused`
- `dread`
- `realization`
- `amazed`
- `defeated`

## Ren (蓮)

An original character with navy hair, amber eyes, a charcoal shirt, and a muted-cyan strap:

- `understands`
- `happy`
- `sad`
- `exhausted`
- `annoyed`
- `frustrated`
- `furious`
- `unhinged`
- `knocked-out`

The names identify the characters, not their musical roles. Either character can be cast as the pianist, guitarist, presenter, or audience stand-in on a slide.

## Kaori (香織)

An original high-energy character with a huge coral-pink ponytail, amber-gold eyes, a charcoal tunic, a cropped magenta jacket, oversized fingerless gauntlets, and a teal belt. Kaori is the cast's intensely theatrical skeptic and gyaru-manga reaction specialist:

- `scandalized` — both palms out, urgently rejecting an obviously terrible idea
- `teasing-wink` — cheeky fang grin and a V sign
- `smug` — folded arms and absolute confidence
- `cackling` — unable to stop laughing
- `tongue-out` — playful eyelid-pull taunt
- `flustered` — crimson denial with crossed arms
- `smitten` — sparkling eyes and helpless adoration
- `ugly-crying` — full waterfall tears
- `pouting` — inflated cheeks and folded arms
- `judging` — merciless side-eye
- `furious` — clenched-fist comedic rage
- `stunned` — blank dot-pupil shock
- `confused` — head tilt, cheek scratch, and shrug
- `exhausted` — completely out of energy
- `scheming` — steepled-finger plotting grin
- `triumphant` — both fists raised in victory
- `pleading` — watery puppy eyes and clasped hands
- `deadpan` — no patience and almost no expression
- `knocked-out` — harmless spiral-eyed defeat

Kaori's set currently uses one strong, slide-readable pose per reaction. Add intensity variants only where a specific slide needs them.
