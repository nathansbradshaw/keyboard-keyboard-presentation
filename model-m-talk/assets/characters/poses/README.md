# Full-body pose catalog

Kaori, Mio, and Ren each have four reusable, text-free full-body cutouts:

- `neutral.png` — relaxed three-quarter standing pose
- `presenting.png` — open-palm explaining or introducing gesture
- `thinking.png` — focused hand-at-chin consideration
- `celebrating.png` — energetic victory pose

The Model M comic interlude also has six scene-specific full-body cutouts:

- `mio/warning-model-m.png` — cautious stop gesture while pointing toward the keyboard
- `kaori/flustered-screwdriver.png` — embarrassed denial with a screwdriver hidden behind her back
- `kaori/confident-model-m.png` — confident recovery presenting the finished modification
- `mio/lovestruck-model-m-warning.png` — blushing, sparkling-eyed warning directed at Ren-senpai
- `mio/tsundere-baka.png` — arms-folded, eyes-averted embarrassed denial with a lingering sideways glance
- `ren/confident-screwdriver.png` — relaxed, self-assured reply with a screwdriver raised

All poses are transparent PNGs with the complete silhouette visible from the highest hair strand to the shoe soles. Kaori is the artistic north star for the set: fine confident dark linework, rich cel contrast, layered hard-edged shadows, glossy highlights, dimensional anatomy, and theatrical slide-readable acting.

Character designs remain consistent with the reaction catalog: Kaori wears her charcoal tunic, cropped magenta jacket, cream shorts, teal belt, gauntlets, and boots; Mio wears her dusty-pink T-shirt, straight-leg light jeans, and white canvas sneakers; Ren wears his brick-red T-shirt, open off-white button-up, cyan strap, dark straight-leg jeans, and black-and-off-white canvas sneakers.
