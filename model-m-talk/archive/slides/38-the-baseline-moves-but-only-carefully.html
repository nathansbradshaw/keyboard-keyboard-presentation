The implementation learns rest at boot from 64 samples. While a key is idle, an IIR baseline follows slow drift at alpha one over 1024, but remains clamped within plus or minus 40 counts of its anchor. HE75 snapshots a manual recalibration. Transition: delta feeds a state machine.
