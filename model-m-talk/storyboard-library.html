Storyboard workflow

Start with the narrative job of the slide. Styling comes after the beat and shot have been chosen.

Build a scene in six decisions

1 · Story beat — what changes?
↓
2 · Camera shot — what should dominate?
↓
3 · Blocking — who or what occupies the stage?
↓
4 · Dialogue — speech, thought, shout, or narration?
↓
5 · Emotion effect — one supporting visual signal
↓
6 · Reveal timing — what appears on each click?

Recommended slide metadata

These attributes make intent visible while authoring and can later power a storyboard overview.

<section class="slide layout-content"
  data-beat="realization"
  data-shot="closeup"
  data-emotion="insight"
  data-transition="firmware implementation">
  ...
</section>

Keep the human delivery note in <aside class="notes">: goal, reveal sequence, caveat, and transition.

03

Anime story beats

These are reusable narrative jobs rather than fixed visual themes.

EYECATCH · ARC 04The KiCad arcRouting begins.
Previously on…

Recap

Restate the last decision and the unresolved consequence. Use at a system boundary or after a long technical section.

Character file

Freeze-frame introduction

Pair one expression with a nameplate, role, and one defining contradiction.

Three weeks later

Time jump

Use a narrator box plus a montage. Show what changed rather than narrating every intermediate step.

System clear

Victory result

State what is now true, the evidence that proves it, and the capability unlocked next.

Next episode

Cliffhanger

End with the next constraint or question. Do not introduce an unrelated teaser.
