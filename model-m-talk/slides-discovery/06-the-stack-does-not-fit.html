The stack does not fit

Melodicade’s mechanism makes sense for an arcade instrument.

Inside a keyboard?

Two complete switches add height, alignment problems, moving parts, and tolerance error.

The idea works. The form factor does not.

This is the emotional reversal. The two-switch measurement is valid, but vertically stacking full mechanisms is too tall and awkward for the keyboard we want to build. We keep the timing insight and the Wicki–Hayden inspiration, but reject the physical implementation. Transition: observe the completely reasonable engineering response.
